# European Union regime facts

## GDPR

- Provide Article 13 information when you collect personal data from the data subject, and provide it when you obtain the data. [EUR-Lex, GDPR Article 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Use only processors that provide sufficient guarantees. Bind processing through a written or electronic contract or other legal act, and require prior specific or general written authorization before the processor appoints another processor. [EUR-Lex, GDPR Article 28](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Maintain written records of processing activities as a controller or processor. Do not use the under-250-person exemption when processing is likely to create risk, is not occasional, includes special-category data, or includes criminal-conviction or offense data. [EUR-Lex, GDPR Article 30](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Notify the supervisory authority of a personal data breach without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours after awareness, unless the breach is unlikely to risk natural persons' rights and freedoms. Require a processor to notify the controller without undue delay, and document every breach. [EUR-Lex, GDPR Article 33](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Notify affected data subjects without undue delay when a personal data breach is likely to create a high risk to their rights and freedoms. Check Article 34(3) before omitting individual notice, including effective protective measures, later risk elimination, or disproportionate effort followed by public communication. [EUR-Lex, GDPR Article 34](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Complete a data protection impact assessment before processing that is likely to create high risk, including systematic and extensive automated evaluation with legal or similarly significant effects or large-scale processing of special-category or criminal data. [EUR-Lex, GDPR Article 35](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

## EU AI Act

- Gate AI Act documents on a covered operator and Union nexus, then classify high risk under Article 6 and Annex III. Document an Article 6(3) conclusion that an Annex III system is not high risk before market placement or service, and treat profiling systems in Annex III as high risk. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Articles 2 and 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

- For a provider of a high-risk AI system, prepare and update technical documentation under Article 11 and Annex IV, then retain the Article 18 provider documentation for 10 years after market placement or service. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Articles 11 and 18 and Annex IV](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

- Require the Article 27 fundamental-rights impact assessment before first deployment by the listed public bodies, public-service entities, and deployers of specified Annex III systems. Cover the deployment process, period and frequency, affected groups, risks, human oversight, governance, and complaint measures. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Article 27](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

- Apply Article 50 transparency duties to covered direct human interaction, machine-readable marking of synthetic content, emotion recognition, biometric categorization, deep fakes, and public-interest text, subject to the article's exceptions. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Article 50](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

- Apply the current phased dates: 2025-02-02 for Chapters I and II except the listed amended Article 5 provisions; 2025-08-02 for the listed governance, GPAI, penalties, and confidentiality provisions; 2026-08-02 as the general date; 2026-12-02 for the listed amended Article 5 provisions; 2027-12-02 for Chapter III Sections 1-3 governing Article 6(2) and Annex III high-risk systems; and 2028-08-02 for those sections governing Article 6(1) and Annex I high-risk systems. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Article 113](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

## Cyber Resilience Act

- Gate Cyber Resilience Act documents on a product with digital elements made available on the Union market whose intended or reasonably foreseeable use includes a direct or indirect logical or physical connection to a device or network. Apply the Regulation from 2027-12-11, Article 14 from 2026-09-11, and Chapter IV from 2026-06-11. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Articles 2 and 71](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Use Annex I as the essential cybersecurity requirements baseline. Cover Part I product properties and Part II manufacturer vulnerability-handling processes. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Annex I](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Use Annex II for user information and instructions, Annexes III and IV to identify important and critical product categories, Annex VI for the simplified EU declaration, and Annex VIII to select the conformity assessment procedure. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Annexes II, III, IV, VI, and VIII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Use Annex V for the EU declaration of conformity, including product and manufacturer identification, sole-responsibility and conformity statements, referenced standards or specifications, notified-body details where applicable, and signature information. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Annex V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.

- Use Annex VII for technical documentation, including the product description, design and development, production and vulnerability-handling processes, cybersecurity risk assessment, support-period basis, applied standards or alternative solutions, test reports, declaration of conformity, and software bill of materials when applicable. [EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Annex VII](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), verified 2026-08-05.
