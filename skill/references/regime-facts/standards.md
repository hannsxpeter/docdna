# Standards

## ISO/IEC 27001

- When ISO/IEC 27001 applies, select the control-mapping register. Determine controls through risk treatment, compare them with Annex A, and record the result. Annex A contains 93 controls grouped as organizational, people, physical, and technological controls. Source: [ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27 Journal 2025, Issue 1](https://committee.iso.org/files/live/sites/jtc1sc27/files/resources/Journal%202025.pdf), verified 2026-08-05.

## AI, requirements, and testing

- When an organization claims an AI management system, select the control-mapping register and AI governance records. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system. Source: [ISO, ISO/IEC 42001:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001), verified 2026-08-05.

- When requirements engineering must follow ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, select the requirements specification and traceability rows. ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 specifies requirements processes, required information items, and their required contents. ISO lists this edition as current and under revision. Source: [ISO, ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html), verified 2026-08-05.

- When testing must follow ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119, select the test-strategy row. Part 2:2021 specifies test processes, and Part 3:2021 specifies test-documentation templates produced by those processes. Sources: [ISO, ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-2:2021](https://www.iso.org/standard/79428.html) and [ISO, ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-3:2021](https://www.iso.org/standard/79429.html), verified 2026-08-05.
