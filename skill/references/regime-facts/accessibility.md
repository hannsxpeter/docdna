# Accessibility

- When EN 301 549 applies, assess against EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (2021-03). For web content, clauses 9.1 to 9.4 include WCAG 2.1 Level A and Level AA criteria, and clause 9.6 tests conformance at Level AA. Source: [ETSI, EN 301 549 V3.2.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/03.02.01_60/en_301549v030201p.pdf), verified 2026-08-05.

- When you claim WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance, require every Level A and Level AA success criterion or a Level AA conforming alternate version. Source: [W3C, WCAG 2.1, section 5.2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#cc1), verified 2026-08-05.

- When a procurement asks for a VPAT, select the Accessibility Conformance Report row and use VPAT 2.5Rev, published April 2025. Choose the INT, Section 508, WCAG, or EU edition that matches the procurement. Source: [Information Technology Industry Council, VPAT resources](https://www.itic.org/policy/accessibility/vpat), verified 2026-08-05.

- Treat an Accessibility Conformance Report as a completed VPAT with documented testing results that details the tested product or service's accessible features. Do not treat the blank template as the report. Source: [Information Technology Industry Council, VPAT](https://www.itic.org/policy/accessibility/vpat), verified 2026-08-05.
