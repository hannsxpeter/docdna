# United States and assurance regime facts

## HIPAA

- Gate HIPAA documents on evidence that you are a covered entity or business associate handling a covered entity's electronic protected health information. [eCFR, 45 CFR 164.302](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-C/section-164.302), verified 2026-08-05.

- Organize Security Rule evidence into administrative safeguards, physical safeguards, and technical safeguards. Map administrative evidence to security management, workforce access, training, incident response, contingency planning, and evaluation; map physical evidence to facilities, workstations, devices, and media; map technical evidence to access control, audit controls, integrity, authentication, and transmission security. [eCFR, 45 CFR 164.308](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-C/section-164.308), [45 CFR 164.310](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-C/section-164.310), and [45 CFR 164.312](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-C/section-164.312), verified 2026-08-05.

- Require an accurate and thorough risk analysis of potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information held by the covered entity or business associate. [eCFR, 45 CFR 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-C/section-164.308), verified 2026-08-05.

- Select a business associate agreement when a covered entity permits a business associate to create, receive, maintain, or transmit electronic protected health information on its behalf. Require the written contract or other arrangement to document the required assurances. [eCFR, 45 CFR 164.308(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-C/section-164.308), verified 2026-08-05.

## SOC 2

- Treat a SOC 2 report as a CPA-issued report on an examination of controls at a service organization that are relevant to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, or privacy. Use it to help user entities and their auditors assess outsourced-service risks and controls. [AICPA, SOC suite](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services) and [AICPA, SOC 2 reporting guide](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/cpe-learning/publication/soc-2-reporting-on-an-examination-of-controls-at-a-service-organization-relevant-to-security-availability-processing-integrity-confidentiality-or-privacy), verified 2026-08-05.

- Apply the common criteria for security to every selected category. Add the category-specific criteria when you select availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, or privacy. Treat those criteria as ASEC-established criteria for evaluating and reporting on controls over information and systems. [AICPA, 2017 Trust Services Criteria with revised 2022 points of focus](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022) and [AICPA, TSP Section 100](https://us.aicpa.org/content/dam/aicpa/interestareas/frc/assuranceadvisoryservices/downloadabledocuments/trust-services-criteria-redlined.pdf), verified 2026-08-05.

## NIST SSDF

- Organize Secure Software Development Framework practices into four groups: Prepare the Organization (PO), Protect the Software (PS), Produce Well-Secured Software (PW), and Respond to Vulnerabilities (RV). Use SP 800-218 as the implementation-neutral secure-development baseline. [NIST CSRC, Secure Software Development Framework](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/ssdf), verified 2026-08-05; [NIST CSRC, SP 800-218](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final), verified 2026-08-05.

## PCI DSS

- Gate PCI DSS documents on evidence that the entity stores, processes, or transmits cardholder data or sensitive authentication data, or can affect the security of the cardholder data environment. [PCI SSC, PCI DSS](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/standards/pci-dss/), verified 2026-08-05.

- For self-assessment, confirm the applicable SAQ with the acquirer or payment brand, scope the environment, complete the assessment, and complete the corresponding Attestation of Compliance. Treat the AOC as the attestation of the organization's PCI DSS compliance status, and submit the requested validation documents to the acquirer or payment brand. [PCI SSC, SAQ steps and AOC](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/1134/), verified 2026-08-05; [PCI SSC, purpose of the AOC](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/1132/), verified 2026-08-05.
