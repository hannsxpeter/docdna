# Artificial intelligence regime facts

## EU AI Act

- For high-risk AI technical documentation, cover all nine Annex IV areas: the system's general description; its elements and development process; monitoring, functioning, and control; performance-metric appropriateness; risk management; lifecycle changes; applied standards or alternative compliance solutions; the EU declaration of conformity; and post-market performance evaluation and monitoring. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Article 11 and Annex IV](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

- For a general-purpose AI model provider, maintain model technical documentation and downstream integration information, adopt a Union copyright-compliance policy, and publish a sufficiently detailed training-content summary using the AI Office template. Apply the Article 53 open-source exception only to the authority and downstream documentation duties, and never apply it to a general-purpose AI model with systemic risk. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Article 53](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

- For a general-purpose AI model with systemic risk, add standardized model evaluation and adversarial testing, Union-level systemic-risk assessment and mitigation, serious-incident tracking and reporting, and adequate cybersecurity for the model and its physical infrastructure. [EUR-Lex, consolidated AI Act, Article 55](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), verified 2026-08-05.

## NIST secure AI development

- Use NIST SP 800-218A with SP 800-218 when producing generative AI or dual-use foundation models or AI systems that use those models. Treat SP 800-218A as the AI-model-specific augmentation to SSDF 1.1, with practices, tasks, recommendations, considerations, notes, and references for secure AI model development. [NIST CSRC, SP 800-218A](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/a/final), verified 2026-08-05.
